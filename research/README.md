### [Seed Round for the Invasion: How the Kremlin Funds and Picks Its Drone Champions](Antonovska-Seed-Round-for-the-Invasion-2026.pdf)
*October 2026 · Policy report · Data as of October 5, 2026*

Russia has built a state-directed substitute for defense venture capital that selects and scales drone "national champions." The report traces the pipeline from state seed grants and venture funds to Defense Ministry validation and state contracts, and shows that Western sanctions reach these firms only after they reach serial production. Using Geoscan as a case study, it documents how ownership restructurings moved key subsidiaries below the U.S. 50 percent threshold, and recommends using the Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act to target the funders that pick Russia's champions.

### [Independence Without Autarky: Why Ending Aid Can Strengthen the U.S.-Israel Alliance](Antonovska-Independence-Without-Autarky-2026.pdf)
*November 2026 · Policy brief*

As Israel moves to phase out U.S. military aid within a decade, this brief argues that ending aid can strengthen the alliance if Israel builds its independence through co-production with the United States rather than self-sufficiency in isolation. It draws a lesson from Russia's drive for defense sovereignty under sanctions, which left Moscow dependent on Iranian designs and Chinese inputs, and recommends a final MOU as a bridge, co-production of munitions and air and missile defense, an updated QME, and Israel's integration into Golden Dome.
